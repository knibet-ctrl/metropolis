# What We Actually Know — The Confidence Ledger

> **The key file.** Everything in this notebook, sorted by how much weight it
> can bear. Read this before arguing with anyone, including yourself.

**The tiers:**

- **TIER 1 — Established.** Multiple independent lines of evidence. Overturning it would require extraordinary evidence.
- **TIER 2 — Contested but tractable.** Serious people disagree; evidence and argument are relevant; progress is possible.
- **TIER 3 — Speculative.** Coherent, unfalsified, unevidenced. Hold loosely.
- **TIER 4 — Unanswerable in principle, or malformed.** No procedure could settle it.

---

## TIER 1 — Established

| Claim | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Mental function depends on brain tissue** | Fine-grained and dose-responsive. Damage a region, lose a specific capacity. The strongest single fact in the notebook |
| **Consciousness can be abolished and restored** | Anaesthesia, deep sleep. Experience is not continuous even within a life |
| **You are physically continuous with the rest of the universe** | Open system, constant material exchange. The boundary is functional, not fundamental |
| **Life on Earth shares common descent** | Genetic, morphological and biogeographical evidence converge |
| **Humans are obligately social** | Isolation causes measurable physiological and psychiatric harm |
| **Memory is reconstructive, not a recording** | Confident, vivid memories can be entirely false |
| **Introspection is unreliable** | Confabulation, blindsight, change blindness. You have far less access to your own processing than it feels like |
| **The universe is very old and very large** | ~13.8 billion years; observable region ~93 billion light years across |
| **Humans are a recent, contingent product of evolution** | No detectable direction or purpose in the process |
| **Cooperation is evolutionarily explicable** | Kin selection, reciprocity, reputation, punishment |
| **Relationships predict health and reported meaning** | Two independent literatures converge on this |
| **Physical processes are either deterministic or random** | Neither delivers libertarian free will |

## TIER 2 — Contested but tractable

| Question | State of play |
|---|---|
| **What is life?** | No agreed definition. Every criterion has counterexamples. Autopoiesis and thermodynamic framings are the strongest candidates |
| **How did life begin?** | Building blocks are easy and common; assembly is unsolved. RNA-world and metabolism-first both live |
| **Do we have free will?** | Compatibilism is the plurality philosophical position. Libertarian free will is near-dead. **The live dispute is about desert, not physics** |
| **What makes a person the same over time?** | Psychological continuity is the mainstream view; Parfit's "identity is not what matters" is the strongest challenge |
| **Is being dead bad for you?** | Epicurus vs the deprivation account. Genuinely unresolved |
| **Is meaning objective, subjective, or hybrid?** | Wolf's hybrid is the strongest available position |
| **How much of the self is socially constructed?** | Substantially. Degree is disputed |
| **Which theory of consciousness is right?** | GWT explains access well. IIT makes testable predictions and is contested. Neither addresses the hard problem |
| **Is the hard problem real or an artefact of introspection?** | The field is genuinely split. This is the crux |

## TIER 3 — Speculative

Hold these loosely. Reasonable people believe them; nobody should be confident.

| Claim | Note |
|---|---|
| **Panpsychism** | Dissolves the emergence problem, creates the combination problem. No longer fringe, still minority |
| **The simulation hypothesis** | Coherent and currently untestable. The probability arguments rest on assumptions that can't be checked |
| **Multiverse** | Several distinct versions from different physics. Mostly not independently testable |
| **Dissipation-driven adaptation** | England's proposal that life is thermodynamically favoured. Interesting, unestablished |
| **Souls** | Unevidenced, no proposed mechanism, and — the sharper objection — no explanatory work left to do |
| **Reincarnation** | Stevenson's case studies are the most serious attempt and are not accepted |
| **Cosmic purpose** | No evidence for or against. Science does not reach it |
| **Machine consciousness** | We have no detector. Both confident positions are unwarranted |
| **Dunbar's number** | Popular, contested, underlying correlation challenged |
| **Terror management theory** | Core idea explains a lot; specific experimental findings have replication problems |

## TIER 4 — Unanswerable in principle, or malformed

| Question | Why it can't be settled |
|---|---|
| **Why is there something rather than nothing?** | Every answer either posits a brute fact or relocates the question. Includes "God did it" and "quantum fluctuation" |
| **Is anyone else conscious?** | Every possible test measures behaviour or physiology. The hard problem *is* that those might occur without experience |
| **What is it like to be a bat?** | Nagel. There are facts we cannot access from outside |
| **Is my experience of red the same as yours?** | No procedure could compare them |
| **What happens after death, as a certainty?** | Best evidence says nothing. Not strictly provable from inside a brain |
| **Is my life meaningful in a cosmic sense?** | Depends on a claim nothing can access |

## The things people most commonly get wrong

Sorted by how often the error appears.

| Belief | Reality |
|---|---|
| "Science has explained consciousness" | It has mapped correlates in detail. Every serious researcher says the hard problem is open |
| "Quantum physics leaves room for free will" | Randomness is not authorship. This is the most common confusion in the topic |
| "Libet proved free will is an illusion" | Trivial tasks, disputed interpretation. The philosophical argument does the work |
| "Every atom replaced every seven years" | Myth. Turnover varies from days to never |
| "Life violates the second law" | Open systems export entropy. Not controversial |
| "The five stages of grief" | Observations of dying patients, not a validated model of bereavement. Not sequential |
| "All religions say the same thing" | They converge on ethics, contradict each other on metaphysics |
| "Buddhism says the self is an illusion" | It denies a *permanent, independent* self. Not the same claim |
| "Just find your passion" | Evidence points to passion following mastery, not preceding it |
| "The Stanford Prison Experiment" | Severe methodological problems. Should not be cited as evidence |
| "Neuroscience found the self in the DMN" | A correlate of self-referential thought, not a self |
| "NDEs prove an afterlife" | They show dying brains produce experiences. Mechanisms exist |
| "We use 10% of our brains" | Complete myth |
| "Most philosophers are hard determinists" | Compatibilism is the plurality position by a wide margin |

## Where the strongest convergences are

When independent methods reach the same conclusion, that's the best signal
available in a domain this soft. There are four.

**1. No persisting self.** Hume (argument, 1739), Buddhist analysis
(introspection, ~5th c. BCE), Parfit (thought experiment, 1984), and
neuroscience (empirical search, ongoing) all fail to find one. **Four
independent routes, same destination.**

**2. Unexamined craving is the main engine of avoidable suffering.** Buddhism,
Stoicism, Christianity, and modern clinical psychology all say some version of
this from entirely different premises.

**3. Interpretation mediates emotional response.** Stoicism said it; CBT
operationalised it and measured it. The clearest ancient-to-modern transmission
in the notebook.

**4. Relationships dominate wellbeing.** The meaning literature and the health
literature converge independently. **Boring, and it is what the data says.**

## Where to spend your attention

The counterintuitive finding, repeated from the-questions.md because it matters
most:

| Question | Feels profound | Changes how you live |
|---|---|---|
| Why is there something rather than nothing? | **Maximum** | **Zero** |
| Is the universe conscious / purposive? | High | Very little — people who believe opposite things live near-identically |
| Free will: libertarian or compatibilist? | High | Little for your choices; **a lot** for how you judge others |
| Is there a persisting self? | Moderate | **Large** — grievance, ambition, fear |
| Is death annihilation? | Moderate | **Large** — urgency |
| Is meaning made or found? | Moderate | **Large** — where you look |
| Do I need other people? | **Low** | **Largest of all** — and it's Tier 1 |

**The pattern: the questions that feel deepest pay the least. The one that pays
the most is the one nobody finds interesting, and it's the only one that's
actually settled.**
